# Disclose regulated-use status without prohibiting use

Cauli will not contractually prohibit regulated workloads during the pilot. Public Legal and Security pages, linked from the public footer and Invitation Activation without a separate acceptance gate, will instead state: “Cauli’s pilot has not been independently assessed, certified, or contractually approved for HIPAA, PCI DSS, FedRAMP, CUI, FERPA, COPPA, GLBA, GDPR-specific, or similar regulated workloads.” Factual descriptions of implemented controls must not imply certification, compliance, readiness, or exemption from laws that may apply, and release checks will reject unsupported regulated-use claims in product and public materials.
