# Security policy

## Report a vulnerability privately

Email [security@cauli.pro](mailto:security@cauli.pro). Do not open a public
GitHub issue or include customer content, credentials, private keys, session
tokens, signed URLs, or unredacted production evidence in a report.

Include a concise description, the affected URL or component, reproduction
steps that use synthetic data, and the potential impact. If sensitive evidence
is necessary, first request a secure transfer method by email.

Cauli will acknowledge Critical reports as quickly as practical and coordinate
remediation and disclosure with the reporter. This policy does not authorize
access to another person’s data, service disruption, social engineering,
physical testing, denial of service, or destructive testing.

## Pilot status

Cauli’s pilot has not been independently assessed, certified, or contractually
approved for HIPAA, PCI DSS, FedRAMP, CUI, FERPA, COPPA, GLBA, GDPR-specific,
or similar regulated workloads. Descriptions of implemented controls do not
represent certification, compliance, regulated-use readiness, or exemption
from obligations that may apply.
